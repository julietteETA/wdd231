NP Membership (Non-Profit)

Annual Cost: Free

  • Listing in the online Chamber directory
  • Invitations to monthly community networking events
  • Access to community bulletin board postings

Bronze Membership

Annual Cost: $250

  • All NP Membership benefits
  • 10% discount on business training workshops
  • Ribbon-cutting ceremony for new businesses
  • Quarterly social media spotlight

Silver Membership

Annual Cost: $500

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • 25% discount on Chamber luncheons and special events
  • Rotating spotlight position on the Chamber home page
  • Complimentary booth at the annual Business Expo

Gold Membership

Annual Cost: $1,000

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • 50% discount on all VIP events and executive training
  • Permanent prime banner advertising on the home page
  • VIP seating at the annual Mayor; Leaders Banquet
